# Watercolour host habitats (9.4.25–9.4.32)

These twelve opaque 1536 × 1024 scenes surrounded the Rätsel from Waldpost 9.4.25 to 9.4.32. They were generated on 22 November 2026 from the registered `puzzle-*` requests and the two canonical watercolour/pencil references.

On 23 November 2026 (9.4.33) the owner replaced them with twelve supplied paintings that keep the same compositions. This folder keeps the exact previous files at their original relative paths. `legacy/manifest.json` records them with their original paths and SHA-256 hashes:

- `assets/forest-friends/puzzle-*.webp`: the shipped runtime exports
- `design/artwork/sources/puzzle-*.png`: the editable generator outputs
- `design/artwork/puzzle-*.json`: their conversion manifests
- `design/artwork/generation-records/puzzle-*.json`: the actual generation requests and ordered references

This is history only. Nothing in the running game, its offline cache or a forward prompt may load or reuse these files. See the [current habitat record](../../../design/artwork/BACKGROUNDS.md).
